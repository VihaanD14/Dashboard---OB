Core Problem Statement
Organisations struggle to identify voluntary attrition drivers at an early, reversible stage. Critical workplace friction remains locked in retrospective exit interviews and siloed departmental records, leaving HR to firefight turnover reactively after key talent has already resigned.
⚠️ The Traditional HR Pathology ("Post-Mortem Fallacy")
  • Prohibitive Turnover Costs: Replacing skilled staff costs 50% to 200% of annual salary (Cascio & SHRM metrics) when accounting for recruitment lag, vacancy productivity loss, and team contagion.
  • The Exit Interview Trap: Exit interviews are conducted after commitment is permanently severed. Departing employees often default to polite concealment ("leaving for higher pay"), masking true operational friction like burnout or stagnant promotion.
  • Stale Annual Surveys: Comprehensive 80-question surveys suffer from response fatigue and 6-month processing lags, making real-time intervention impossible.
💡 The RetAIn Solution: Continuous Closed-Loop Telemetry
  • Connecting Historical Exits to Active Cohorts: Instead of filing exit feedback away, RetAIn uses NLP to mine past exit narratives and dynamically monitor matching warning signs across currently active teams.
  • Targeted Micro-Pulse Diagnostics: Rather than blasting broad company-wide surveys, the platform fires hyper-focused 2–3 question micro-pulses directly to teams exhibiting preliminary distress signals.
  • Predictive to Prescriptive: Automatically recommends actionable, budget-conscious retention tactics tailored to existing internal company HR policies.
Operational Architecture: How RetAIn Solves Attrition End-to-end data pipeline from diagnostic signals to financial ROI
Stage 01
Exit Intelligence & NLP Root-Cause

Ingests unstructured exit feedback. Identifies the Polite Concealment Gap by cross-checking standardized dropdown reasons against qualitative sentiment to pinpoint true systemic triggers.

Stage 02
Triggered Micro-Pulse Telemetry

Deploys psychometrically validated 2–3 question micro-pulses to active teams. Eliminates survey fatigue while testing whether departed colleagues' concerns are actively propagating.

Stage 03
Deterministic Risk Scoring (0–100)

Computes a formulaic Flight Risk Index combining inverted sentiment (40%), trailing exit velocity (35%), and tenure stagnation (25%). Deterministic math calculates the score; AI solely explains it.

Stage 04
Policy Actions & ROI Attribution

Maps remedies onto a 2×2 Cost vs. Impact matrix (Quick Wins vs. Strategic Moves), cross-referenced with enterprise policies. Computes net cost savings and breakeven exits over 30/60/90 days.

🛡️ Enterprise-Grade Differentiators for Industry Evaluators
▸
Zero AI Hallucinations in Risk Scoring: The 0–100 Flight Risk Index is computed via transparent, deterministic Python formulas. Generative AI is strictly restricted to qualitative synthesis and policy interpretation.
▸
Privacy-by-Design & Psychological Safety: Strict cohort-level aggregation (k-anonymity threshold of n ≥ 5). The system never generates individual flight risk labels, fully complying with India's DPDP Act 2023.
▸
Controlled Intervention Attribution: Evaluates intervention success by subtracting comparison control cohort drops from treated cohort drops, proving whether attrition fell due to HR action or macro trends.
